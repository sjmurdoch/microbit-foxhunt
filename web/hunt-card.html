A Fox is hidden somewhere in the area. It makes no sound and shows no light — it is whispering by radio. Your Hound hears that whisper and tells you how loud it is, but never which way it came from. Find the Fox.

Three tricks that win it

1. Walk and watch

Pick a direction and walk twenty paces. Did the beeps speed up, or slow down?

Faster → keep going. Slower → turn around.

Slow = far off · medium = warm · fast alarm = very close. The signal fades with distance, so the beeps are really a distance meter.

2. Turn on the spot

Stand still, hold the Hound in front of you and turn slowly all the way round. Watch for the reading to drop.

When it drops, the Fox is behind you.

Your own body is in the way. Radio does not go through people easily — and your Hound's aerial listens every way at once, so blocking it is the only way to get a direction.

3. Sunglasses

When every bar is lit and nothing changes as you move, press button A. Press it again if it fills up again. B undoes it.

A only changes the Hound, never the Fox — like putting sunglasses on in bright light. It lets you tell two metres from one.

Quick think — two minutes at the end

  1. How did you work out which way to go?
  2. What happened to the beeps as you got closer?
  3. When the screen filled up, what did you do — and what did pressing A actually change?

Finished early? Hide the Fox yourself and time the next team — near a wall is harder, because radio bounces off solid things.

Walk, do not run. Stay inside the boundary. Never swing a board by its battery wire.
