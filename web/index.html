🦊 Fox Hunt flasher

Puts the fox or the hound onto a BBC micro:bit V2, straight from this page.

This browser cannot flash a micro:bit. Flashing over USB needs Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS. Firefox and Safari do not support it, and neither does any browser on iPad or iPhone.
You can still use the hex downloads below and drag them onto the MICROBIT drive.

1. Choose a radio group

The fox and the hound must be on the same group, and each hunt running at the same time needs its own. If they differ, the hound simply never hears anything — there is no error to see, so write the group on a sticker and put it on both boards.

2. Connect a board

Plug one micro:bit in with a USB cable that carries data — a charge-only cable is the commonest reason nothing appears. Connect one board at a time.

3. Flash it

Radio monitor

This replaces the game on the connected board with a listener that prints what it hears. Put the fox somewhere on its battery, then watch below. Use Put the game back when you are finished.

Running a hunt

Before you start
  • Fresh or fully charged AAA cells in every board. The fox must last the whole session.
  • Fox and every hound on the same radio group. Label them.
  • Check the fox is dark — its display should be blank. That is how it hides.
  • Walk a hound 20 m from the fox and confirm it still beeps before you hide anything.
  • Agree the boundaries, a finish time, and what to do if someone gets lost.
What the players hear and see
  • Slow beep — far away. Medium — getting warm. Fast alarm — very close.
  • The LED bars show signal strength. They rise as you get closer.
  • Button A turns the sensitivity down when the screen fills up, so you can keep tracking the last few metres. Button B resets it.
  • The aerial points everywhere at once, so there is no direction arrow. Turn on the spot: your own body blocks the signal, so when the reading dips, the fox is behind you.
What the ranges should look like

Measured outdoors on 2026-09-06. Your field will differ; these are the shape, not a promise.

DistanceWhat the hound does
1–3 mFast alarm. Only about one bar of change across these three metres — use button A and body shielding, not the bars.
~8 mDanger zone gone, middle zone at its fringe.
15–20 mSlow beep only. The display may go blank around 15 m while the sound keeps working — that is expected, not a fault.
If something is wrong
SymptomLikely cause
Hound is completely silentDifferent radio group, out of range, or the fox's battery is flat. Check the group first — it is the only one of the three with no other symptom.
Screen blank but still beepingNormal at longer range. Keep walking; the bars come back.
Screen full, no sense of directionPress button A to add attenuation.
Board does nothing after flashingReflash it. If it was flashed from a downloaded .hex, check it is a V2.
Nothing appears when connectingCharge-only USB cable, or another tab or program already has the board.
Command line says “board busy” right after using this pageClose this tab or just try again — it settles on its own.
