🦊 **The Great Radio Fox Hunt!**

**Your Mission**
A hidden transmitter (the "Fox") is broadcasting an invisible radio signal somewhere in the area. Your job is to use your receiver (the "Hound") to track down the Fox's hiding spot!

**The Science of Radio 📡**
Radio waves are exactly like invisible light. Imagine the Fox is a flashlight shining in the fog. 
- When you are far away, the light is very dim. 
- As you get closer, the light gets blindingly bright.
Your Hound measures how "bright" this invisible radio signal is so you can follow it to the source. But watch out—radio waves can bounce off metal and walls, creating tricky echoes!

**How to Track the Fox 🐾**
1. **Listen to the Beeps**: The Fox is broadcasting 3 different invisible "zones". 
   - *Slow Beep*: You are far away (Outer Zone).
   - *Medium Beep*: You are getting warm (Middle Zone).
   - *Fast Alarm*: You are super close! (Danger Zone).
2. **Watch the Screen**: The LED lights show the exact signal strength. Move around and turn your body. The lights will go up when you step toward the Fox.

**The Pro-Tracker Trick (The Attenuator) 🎛️**
If you get close to the Fox, the signal becomes so "bright" that all 5 rows of lights will turn on. When the screen is full, you can't tell if you're getting warmer! 
- Press **Button A** to "wear sunglasses." This adds *attenuation* (making the Hound less sensitive) so the lights drop down, letting you track the final few steps! You can press A multiple times.
- Press **Button B** to take the sunglasses off and reset the meter to normal.

**Safety & Equipment Rules ⚠️**
* **Walk, don't run!** You need to look at the screen, which means you aren't looking where you are going.
* **Protect the Hound**: The micro:bits are fragile electronic circuit boards. Hold them securely, keep them dry, and *never* swing them by the battery wire!
* **Stay in Bounds**: Only hunt within the designated game area. Have fun!
